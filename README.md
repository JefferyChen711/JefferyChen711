## Hi, I'm Jeffery 👋

I work where data meets decisions: figuring out what the numbers say, what to build, and what to do next. My background spans **business & data analytics, data science, product, and strategy consulting** across tech, automotive, sports & entertainment.

### 🎯 What I do

- **Analytics & BI**: turning messy, multi-source data into dashboards and insights leaders actually use
- **Data Science**: predictive modeling, forecasting, and causal inference (A/B tests, DiD, RDD, causal forests)
- **Product & GenAI**: defining success metrics, LLM evaluation (LLM-as-judge), and agentic workflows
- **Strategy**: pricing, segmentation, market entry, and ROI analysis that drives business calls
